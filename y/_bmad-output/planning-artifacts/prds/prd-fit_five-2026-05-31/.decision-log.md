## Decisions Captured

1. FiveMatch is a native mobile app for Apple App Store and Google Play Store, not only a hosted web app.
2. MVP focuses on private groups and two-team match generation.
3. Future architecture should remain open for group-vs-group matches, city leagues, and tournaments.
4. Ratings use a 0-100 scale.
5. Initial peer ratings seed the baseline once; the algorithm evolves ratings after that.
6. Team generation is blocked until every confirmed match player has at least 3 initial peer ratings.
7. Match attendance uses first-come confirmed spots and waitlist for MVP.
8. Group settings include language, play style, intensity, goalkeeper mode, and default generator style.
9. MVP generator styles are Fair + Fresh, Maximum Fairness, and More Variety.
10. Teams are locked once organizer/admin confirms a team generation version.
11. Regeneration is allowed before confirmation.
12. One limited manual adjustment flow is allowed before confirmation and should not be promoted.
13. Organizer/admin enters score, goals, and assists for MVP.
14. Players vote MVP and can quickly rate opponents after matches.
15. Opponent ratings are skippable but not recommended.
16. Ratings can go up and down.
17. Fun/personality data supports cards and social identity, not competitive balance.
18. All group members can view results and generate available share assets.
19. MVP share assets are live player card, MVP card, and match result poster.
20. Player cards are live in MVP, not historical collectible snapshots.
21. Prompt-based Panini-style card generation is important.
22. Card image generation gets one validated attempt.
23. French and English are required.
24. Push notifications are part of MVP.
25. Recommended MVP navigation is Accueil, Joueurs, Match, Vestiaire, Profil.
26. Baseline rating values are anonymous by default; progress counts are visible.
27. Initial peer ratings can be edited until used in a confirmed team generation version.
28. Maybe affects reminders only and does not influence waitlist priority.
29. MVP allows up to 5 generated team versions before confirmation.
30. Before score submission, organizer/admin can cancel locked teams and reopen team generation.
31. After score submission, casual team changes are not allowed.
32. Magic link authentication is deferred unless provided cheaply by the auth provider.
33. Pronostics is deferred or hidden for MVP.
34. MVP supports minimum playable format 4v4 and recommends 5v5 as the default first match format.
35. MVP includes one free successful generated card image attempt per player profile per group.
36. Technical card generation failures do not consume the attempt; aesthetic retries are deferred.
37. Uploaded photos and prompts must pass automated safety checks before generation.
38. Score and match events can be edited until match completion finalization.
39. After completion finalization, owner/admin can void a match; voided matches are excluded from leaderboards and rating evolution.
40. PRD review created at `review-rubric.md`.
41. PRD now includes explicit Product Thesis and MVP Trade-offs.
42. PRD glossary now defines Owner, Admin, Organizer, Generator Style, Match Completion Finalization, and Voided Match.
43. PRD now includes cross-cutting NFRs for responsiveness, data integrity, privacy, image safety, localization, notifications, and accessibility.
44. Brainstorm input reconciliation created at `reconcile-brainstorm.md`.
45. Remaining open questions are non-blocking tuning questions with owners and revisit conditions.
46. PRD status set to final on 2026-05-31.
